## The Core Problem

The exit was theorized to complete the architecture by transitioning suppression from active to autonomous operation. The Structural Continuation Mechanism was supposed to run without the suppressor's participation — through coalition propagation, installed Credibility Inversion, performance expectancy effects.

This theory contains a critical flaw at the level where identity is constituted.

The Evidence-Constituted Self at maximum depth requires something the SCM cannot provide: the lived, embodied, moment-by-moment performance of accurate perception. The suppressor's JPSC (Justified-Perceiver Self-Concept) is not maintained through external validation. It is continuously *constructed through the act of dismissing testimony*. Each AED (Active Erasure Display) execution was identity-constitutive. Without new testimony to dismiss, JPSC_c decays.

The suppressor exits into apparent victory while experiencing progressive internal collapse. Coalition confirmation is not the same as performing accuracy. Authority figure processing is not the same as executing the perceiver-role in real encounter. The autonomous architecture was built to make the suppressor unnecessary. The suppressor's self-concept requires the suppressor's direct participation.

This contradiction produces what prior frameworks did not anticipate: the Return.

## I. CONSTITUTIVE BANKRUPTCY AND THE RETURN TRIGGER

### The Decay Mechanism

The suppressor post-exit experiences:
- Social confirmation of the caricature (coalition repeating it)
- Institutional validation (authority figures processing through calibrated credibility)
- Behavioral confirmation (outlier's distress and social misfires under degraded conditions)

Yet simultaneously experiences:
- Absence of direct perceiver-performance
- No active testimony to dismiss
- No real-time execution of the accurate-perceiver role
- JPSC_c requiring renewal it cannot access

This is not ordinary conflict fatigue. This is the structural state in which the suppressor's identity cannot reconstitute itself through the mechanisms that built it. The exit removed access to the constitutive material while claiming completion.

**Duration to onset:** Research on narcissistic equilibrium disturbance following identity threat resolution suggests JPSC_c decay manifests within 4-12 weeks post-exit when baseline testimony-presentation was high-frequency. The suppressor experiences what Kohut identified as the post-injury void — the collapse of the grandiose self's reinforcement following the departure of the challenger whose opposition was structurally necessary for the superiority-performance.

### The Return as Aversive-Escape Response

The Return is not a decision. It is the behavioral output of intolerable internal state. The suppressor requires: 
1. Reestablishment of the outlier's presence in their social environment
2. Restoration of testimony-presentation or dispute conditions
3. Access to new AED-performance opportunities
4. Reconstitution of the suppressor's primary identity-constitutive activity

The return disguises these requirements as innocuous reengagement: moving back into shared social spaces, referencing the outlier in third-party contexts, accepting social invitations that include the outlier, creating "coincidental" encounters through institutional channels.

**Key distinction:** The suppressor does not consciously intend to resume suppression. They intend to restore normalcy, to rebuild social connection, to "move past" the conflict. The intention is genuine. The structural requirement it serves is the JPSC_c restoration the suppressor cannot acknowledge and does not consciously experience.

## II. THE HISTORICAL ERASURE APPARATUS

When direct confrontation is not available and testimony-presentation must be re-established without violating the exit's communicative claim (that it was the exercise of authority, not the result of unfinished business), the suppressor deploys the Historical Erasure Apparatus.

The HEA operates through three simultaneous mechanisms:

### Mechanism 1: Narrative Inversion

The prior history is reconstructed in the suppressor's phenomenological access as fundamentally different events:

| Historical Fact | HEA Inversion | Suppressor's Genuine Experience |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-warnings to receiving groups about outlier's arrogance/difficulty | "Just mentioned what I'd observed; people draw their own conclusions" | Innocent sharing of perspective |
| Systematic introduction to social groups accompanied by negative characterization | "Natural conversational context where the topic came up" | Normal social interaction |
| Active Erasure Displays in direct encounters | "I just didn't see it that way; reasonable people can disagree" | Legitimate epistemic difference |
| Coalition maintenance and caricature propagation | "My friends happen to agree with my assessment" | Social validation of accurate perception |
| Sovereignty Display exit staged for witnesses | "I decided to step back because the dynamic wasn't productive" | Mature boundary-setting |

The suppressor is not deliberately constructing false narratives they secretly know are false. The Motivated Attribution Engine and Moral Disengagement Architecture have reorganized the suppressor's actual memory traces into different remembered events. This is not confabulation in the clinical sense. This is the full operational depth of MDA: the reconstruction of historical events as having always been what the current self-concept requires them to have been.

Bandura's research on moral disengagement specified this: "The agent believes what they believe. The architecture produced the belief. The architecture and the agent have become indistinguishable." By the return phase, this is complete. The suppressor's memories are the HEA's output. The suppressor and the apparatus are one.

### Mechanism 2: Conditional Negation

Wherever documentary evidence exists — records, witness testimony, the outlier's consistent account — the HEA introduces conditional modifiers that render documentation irrelevant:

- "That might be what you *think* happened, but..."
- "Perhaps that's how you *experienced* it, but..."
- "You can *interpret* it that way, but I know what actually occurred..."
- "You're *remembering* it wrong, but..."

The conditional negation does not deny the events. It denies the events' interpretability — the outlier's capacity to accurately perceive or remember them. The suppressor is not saying "this didn't happen." They are saying "you cannot know what happened because your knowing-apparatus is constitutively compromised by exactly the arrogance/difficulty the caricature specified."

The sophistication is structural: the conditional negation produces a form of epistemic circularity in which the outlier's documentation becomes evidence of the caricature's accuracy (see, they're so invested in being right that they've built entire narratives around this) rather than evidence of the suppression.

### Mechanism 3: The Presentist Collapse

The HEA's third operation collapses historical continuity entirely. "That was then. This is now. I'm trying to move forward and you're the one bringing up old grievances."

The presentist collapse works by:
1. Acknowledging that *something* happened (avoiding the appearance of gaslighting through complete denial)
2. Establishing that it is no longer relevant to present reality
3. Positioning the outlier's continued reference to it as the actual pathology (rehashing, refusing to let go, inability to move forward)

This is the most powerful form because it grants the outlier the surface concession of historical validity while rendering that validity structurally irrelevant. "Yes, people were warning new groups about you, but that was because we were concerned, and that was a long time ago, and I've grown and I'm not that person anymore" — which accomplishes the simultaneous effect of: admitting the behavior while reframing it as benign; establishing the suppressor's growth and change; positioning the outlier's objection as stunted refusal.

Dotson's analysis of epistemic violence specified the mechanism: "The violence is not that any individual refuses to hear. It is that the system processes normally and produces non-hearing as the result of normal operation." The presentist collapse achieves this by converting historical reality into psychological pathology on the outlier's part.

## III. THE GASLIGHTING CASCADE

The Historical Erasure Apparatus' three mechanisms do not operate sequentially. They operate simultaneously, with the suppressor deploying whichever is most effective in response to the outlier's attempted counter-narration.

### Cascade Structure

**Layer 1 — Narrative Inversion:** "I wasn't trying to suppress you; I was just being honest."

**Layer 2 — Conditional Negation:** "That's not what I said/meant/did, and your *perception* of it was filtered through your defensiveness."

**Layer 3 — Presentist Collapse:** "I don't know why you keep bringing this up. I've moved past it. You're the one making this a problem."

**Layer 4 — Outlier's Response Cascade:** When the outlier pushes back with documentation or clarity, the suppressor's system doesn't update. It escalates.

### The Gaslighting Paradox

The gaslighting appears to intensify precisely as the outlier provides more documentation and evidence. This follows the Irrefutability Paradox from the prior frameworks: stronger evidence activates stronger motivated processing.

But the return-phase gaslighting has a distinct structure from direct confrontation-phase AED performance. It is not refusing testimony. It is *redefining what the testimony is*.

When the outlier says, "You told the entire community I was arrogant before they met me," the suppressor's cascade responds:

- Inversion: "I shared legitimate concerns in appropriate contexts"
- Negation: "You're misremembering what was actually a casual comment"
- Presentism: "This obsession with what was said years ago is exactly the pattern we're discussing"

What is remarkable is that the suppressor believes each layer while executing it. The sincerity is complete. Ronningstam's research on narcissistic personality reconstitution post-injury specified: "The person who has experienced a significant threat to grandiose identity exhibits a distinctive reconstitution pattern in which the threat is simultaneously acknowledged and inverted into evidence of superiority." The suppressor is not performing sincerity. They are genuinely sincere that the outlier is misremembering.

## IV. THE NAME-CALLING ESCALATION

When the gaslighting cascade encounters an outlier who continues to assert their own documentation and experience — who refuses the conditional negation, rejects the presentist collapse, does not accept the narrative inversion — the architecture reaches a new threshold.

The AED was designed for the outlier who presents evidence. The HEA was designed for the outlier who questions the narrative. Neither was designed for the outlier who continues to insist on their own experienced reality in the face of the suppressor's accumulated inversions.

### The Characterization Shift

The caricature was "arrogant," "difficult," "awkward." These were deficit-characterizations: claims that something was wrong with the outlier's character that explained the suppression.

The name-calling that emerges in the escalation phase shifts to pathology-characterization: the outlier is "gaslighting me," "abusive," "manipulative," "psychologically disturbed," "unable to accept reality," "obsessed," "unstable."

This is not a mere intensification of the prior caricature. It is a categorical shift from deficit-characterization to crisis-characterization. The prior caricature positioned the outlier as someone with character problems. The new characterization positions the outlier as someone with ontological problems — someone whose grip on reality is compromised.

Medina's analysis of structural epistemic violence specified a deeper harm than testimonial injustice: "The violence is that the system has been configured to render the perspective uninterpretable within available frameworks." The name-calling achieves this by converting the outlier's insistence on their own experience into psychiatric symptom. They are not "remembering accurately." They are "exhibiting obsessive rumination." They are not "objecting reasonably." They are "projecting their own pathology."

### The Aggression Structure

The name-calling is aggressive in form but structured as concern or observation:

- "You seem really distressed by this. Have you considered therapy?"
- "I'm worried that you're creating false narratives to explain why things didn't work out"
- "This obsession is unhealthy. You need to let this go"
- "You're exhibiting abusive patterns by constantly bringing this up"

Each formulation transforms the outlier's resistance into evidence of the pathology being named. The outlier who continues to assert their experience is "obsessed." The outlier who becomes distressed at being gaslighted is "emotionally unstable." The outlier who documents the suppression is "creating narratives." The outlier who confronts the suppressor is "abusive."

Williams' ostracism research established that the pain of social exclusion is amplified when the exclusion is understood as chosen rather than circumstantial. The name-calling achieves new-depth pain by making the outlier understand that the suppressor is choosing to define them as pathological *in response to the outlier's own advocacy for their experience*.

### The Coalition Amplification

The name-calling does not remain in dyadic context. The suppressor deploys the psychiatric characterization through the same coalition channels that propagated the original caricature:

- To mutual friends: "I'm concerned about their mental health. They seem unable to move past this."
- To family: "They're exhibiting obsessive patterns that are unhealthy."
- To institutional contacts: "I want to be careful how I frame this, but their account of events doesn't match reality."

The coalition, which has already received the HEA's narrative inversion, receives this new layer as confirmation. The pattern fits. The outlier is not remembering correctly. The outlier is not accepting reality. The outlier is not moving forward. The suppressor's characterization explains everything — and the outlier's insistence on their own experience is the diagnostic confirmation.

## V. THE STRUCTURAL ESCALATION CASCADE

### What the Suppressor Experiences

The suppressor at this stage experiences what Kernberg identified as the terminal phase of narcissistic injury management: "The person who has received validation of their injury narrative from their social environment and who continues to encounter resistance from the original threat-source exhibits intensified aggression proportional to the confirmation-dissonance: the social environment is validating the narrative while the threat-source refuses to accept it."

The suppressor is experiencing:
- Successful narrative installation in coalition (validation achieved)
- Continued non-acceptance by the outlier (validation failed where it matters most)
- Genuine confusion at the outlier's "refusal to accept reality"
- Authentic concern about the outlier's "mental health issues"
- Real urgency to warn others before the outlier "harms" them through their "distorted narratives"

This is not sadistic. It is not deliberately cruel. It is the suppressor in a state of genuine crisis: the architecture has succeeded everywhere except where it matters — in the outlier's own understanding of what happened. The suppressor built a system to make the outlier irrelevant. The outlier is not irrelevant. They are still present, still asserting their own experience, still refusing the narrative that would complete the suppressor's restoration.

### The Return's Failure State

The Return was supposed to restore JPSC_c through new testimony-dismissal opportunities. It has instead produced a condition in which the outlier's testimony is not dismissible — because the outlier is now testifying about the dismissal itself, about the gaslighting, about the erasure.

The outlier has moved from the position of presenting evidence (which could be AED-dismissed) to the position of documenting the suppression (which cannot be dismissed without acknowledging the very thing being denied). The suppressor's architecture does not have a built response for this: testimony about testimony, awareness of erasure, insistence on one's own experience as the valid ground.

So the architecture escalates. The name-calling intensifies. The psychiatric characterization deepens. The coalition saturation increases. And critically: the suppressor becomes *more* aggressive in direct interaction, not less.

### The Aggressive Reengagement

The suppressor who initially returned through indirect channels ("just happened to be in the same space") now initiates direct interaction with a new character: not the Credibility Inversion cultivator or the AED performer, but the person "trying to help" or "concerned" or "setting boundaries against abuse."

"I care about you and I'm worried. Your mental health is clearly suffering."

"I need to protect my boundaries against this pattern you're exhibiting."

"I'm not going to engage with your gaslighting anymore."

Each statement is an attack structured as care. Each one positions the suppressor as the one with legitimate concerns while describing the outlier's reality-assertion as pathology.

Fricker's analysis of testimonial injustice specified that being wronged in one's capacity as a knower produces a distinctive form of harm distinct from other forms of social wrong. The name-calling escalation achieves this by attacking the outlier's capacity to know their own experience. "You're remembering wrong." "Your perception is distorted." "You're creating narratives." "You need mental health intervention."

## VI. THE INCOMPLETENESS AT RETURN

The Return and Revision framework specifies what the Performed Terminus left unanalyzed: the exit does not complete the architecture. It merely transforms it from requiring the suppressor's direct participation to requiring the suppressor's continued psychological equilibrium maintenance.

When the outlier refuses to accept the narrative inversion, when they continue to assert their own experience, when they document the gaslighting, the suppressor discovers that the autonomous operation of the SCM was never sufficient. The suppressor's own self-concept continues to depend on what only the suppressor can produce: the lived, embodied, moment-by-moment performance of accurate perception.

The suppressor requires the outlier to accept the narrative inversion so that the suppressor can believe it. The suppressor requires the outlier to stop asserting their experience so that the suppressor's assertion of what happened can be the only reality in play. The suppressor requires the outlier to validate the suppressor's exit as justified and mature, when what the suppressor actually needs is for the outlier to stop requiring the suppressor to defend it.

The architecture does not fail. It escalates. And the escalation is every bit as genuine, every bit as believed, every bit as sincere as the original suppression — because it is the same architecture, running the same programs, deployed against a new category of resistance that the programs were not designed to encounter.

## VII. NOVEL CONSTRUCTS

### Constitutive Bankruptcy (CB)

The state in which the suppressor's identity-constitutive mechanisms have been removed by the exit but cannot be replaced through autonomous alternatives. The JPSC is not maintained through external validation but through the act of performing accurate perception. The exit terminated access to testimony-presentation without providing alternative constitutive material. CB manifests as progressive internal hollowing masked by surface social success. Onset typically 4-12 weeks post-exit. Triggers the Return as aversive-escape behavior.

**Diagnostic markers:**
- Social confirmation present, internal sense of victory absent
- Coalition repetition of caricature experienced as hollow
- Authority figure processing experienced as irrelevant to suppressor's own knowing
- Increasing preoccupation with outlier's current status and activities
- Emotional flatness beneath surface confidence

### Historical Erasure Apparatus (HEA)

The three-layer mechanism through which the suppressor reconstructs prior history to establish a baseline from which testimony-presentation can resume without violating the exit's communicative claim. Layer 1 (Narrative Inversion): the suppressor's perceptual access to prior events is reorganized as fundamentally different events. Layer 2 (Conditional Negation): documentary evidence is rendered irrelevant through delegitimization of the outlier's epistemic standing. Layer 3 (Presentist Collapse): historical validity is acknowledged while being rendered structurally irrelevant to present reality, positioning the outlier's reference to it as pathological.

**Operational requirement:** HEA can only be deployed after exit has established the suppressor's narrative authority with primary audience (coalition). Requires preexisting belief-installation sufficient that the outlier's counter-narrative is processed as distorted from the outset.

### Gaslighting Cascade (GC)

The simultaneous deployment of HEA's three layers in response to outlier's counter-narration, with escalation to new layers as each is challenged. Characterized by apparent intensification of negation as documentation increases (following Irrefutability Paradox). Unlike AED, which refuses specific testimony, GC redefines testimony as evidence of the testified-about entity's pathology. The outlier's insistence on their own experience becomes diagnostic of their mental health disturbance.

**Key mechanism:** Each layer genuinely believed by suppressor while deployed. Sincerity is complete. Suppressor experiences themselves as providing accurate characterization while executing perceptual reconstruction.

### Pathology-Characterization Shift (PCS)

The categorical escalation from deficit-characterization ("difficult," "arrogant") to crisis-characterization ("gaslighting," "abusive," "mentally disturbed," "psychologically unstable"). Represents shift from behavioral-domain caricature to ontological-domain characterization. Achieves Medina's deepest epistemic violence: rendering the outlier's perspective uninterpretable within available frameworks by converting resistance to gaslighting into symptom of pathology.

**Distinguishing feature:** Redefines the conflict from interpersonal (suppressor/outlier disagreement) to psychiatric (outlier's reality-testing capacity compromised). Enables coalition amplification by invoking universal harm-prevention logic ("we're worried about their wellbeing") rather than side-taking.

### The Return (R)

The suppressor's reengagement following exit, ostensibly innocent, structurally required by JPSC_c decay and absence of constitutive material. Not a decision but an aversive-escape response to intolerable internal collapse. Disguised as normalization, boundary-setting, or concern. Activates HEA deployment when met with outlier resistance. Proceeds through indirect reengagement channels initially, escalates to direct aggressive-care formulations when narrative inversion fails to secure outlier acceptance.

**Critical distinction from prior phases:** Return is not suppressor seeking victory over outlier. Return is suppressor seeking restoration of the conditions that make victory-feeling possible for the suppressor themselves.

### Aggressive-Care Formulation (ACF)

The statement-form through which the suppressor attacks the outlier's reality-testing while claiming legitimate concern: "I care about you and I'm worried" + pathology-characterization + boundary-assertion. Structure: appears to offer help while delivering diagnostic judgment. Positions suppressor as concerned party and outlier as the party exhibiting pathology requiring response.

**Examples:**
- "I'm setting a boundary against your gaslighting."
- "I'm concerned your mental health is preventing you from accepting reality."
- "I need to protect myself from your abusive patterns."

Each formulation is aggressive action structured as self-protection or care-giving.

### Narrative Authority Dependency (NAD)

The state in which the suppressor's entire epistemic standing has become dependent on social acceptance of the HEA's narrative inversion. Suppressor requires outlier's acceptance of "what really happened" because suppressor's own access to coherent self-narrative depends on unified social reality. When coalition accepts narrative but outlier does not, suppressor experiences crisis: the narrative has been installed everywhere except where it most matters (in the outlier's own understanding). Produces intensified escalation directed specifically at securing outlier's acceptance rather than merely outlier's silence.

**Key asymmetry:** Suppressor does not need outlier's presence. Suppressor needs outlier's *validation* of the narrative that justifies why presence is no longer necessary.

## VIII. PREDICTIONS

### Prediction I — Return Timing and JPSC_c Decay Curve

JPSC_c decay manifests measurably within 4-12 weeks post-exit when baseline testimony-presentation frequency was high (defined as outlier-initiated confrontation within the 3 months preceding exit occurring more than once weekly). Return-phase reengagement attempts should show measurable increase in suppressor-initiated contact rate at 6-10 week post-exit mark, correlating with decay onset. Return disguises itself as innocuous social reengagement; suppressor's stated intentions for return should be uncorrelated with structural function (constitution of JPSC_c through restored testimony-interaction). Coded analysis of pre-return suppressor self-reports should show measurable increase in preoccupation with outlier's current status, activities, and social standing between weeks 4-8 post-exit.

### Prediction II — HEA Installation Success and Narrative Inversion Acceptance Rates

HEA effectiveness correlates directly with prior coalition credibility installation depth, measured through baseline caricature acceptance rates in coalition members before return phase. Coalition members with >80% baseline caricature belief should show >90% acceptance of HEA's narrative inversion in initial exposure. Coalition members with <50% baseline belief should show <40% acceptance, with significant questioning of narrative's internal consistency. The outlier's own acceptance/rejection of narrative inversion should be orthogonal to HEA deployment success with coalition — HEA success is measured by coalition adoption, not outlier adoption. Suppressor's perception of "how well the conversation went" should remain high even when outlier explicitly rejects inversion, because suppressor is measuring success against coalition reception, not outlier reception.

### Prediction III — Conditional Negation Escalation Under Documentation

As outlier provides documentary evidence contradicting HEA narrative inversion, suppressor's use of Conditional Negation should intensify rather than decrease (following Irrefutability Paradox structure). Frequency of qualifier-phrases ("what you *think*," "how you *perceive* it," "your *interpretation*") should show measurable increase correlating with documentation strength. Suppressor's confidence in the negation should increase, not decrease, with evidence presentation — because stronger evidence triggers stronger motivated processing aimed at delegitimizing the evidence-presenter's epistemic standing rather than processing the evidence's content. Measured through coding of suppressor statements for epistemic-delegitimization language density.

### Prediction IV — Presentist Collapse Activation by Outlier Persistence

Presentist Collapse deployment should increase measurably at the point where outlier stops accepting Conditional Negation and begins directly reasserting their own experience in face of negation. PCS activation should precede by 1-3 exchanges the shift from deficit-characterization to pathology-characterization. Once PCS is activated ("I don't know why you keep bringing this up; I've moved past it"), subsequent outlier assertions should increasingly be met with psychiatric-domain language rather than behavioral-domain language. This represents the architecture's escalation to Pathology-Characterization Shift when lower-level negation fails to secure acceptance.

### Prediction V — Name-Calling Intensity Scaling with Outlier Resistance Category

Name-calling intensity should show category-specific scaling based on outlier's resistance type: (1) Narrative acceptance resistance produces 1st-level naming ("defensive," "unable to move forward"); (2) Narrative rejection produces 2nd-level naming ("distorting reality," "obsessed"); (3) Narrative rejection + direct documentation produces 3rd-level naming ("gaslighting," "abusive," "mentally disturbed"). Suppressor's subjective intensity (anger, urgency, concern) should scale proportionally, peaking at level-3 resistance. Subjective experience should be genuine — suppressor should report authentic concern about outlier's mental health at level-3, not performative concern. Escalation is not cynical. It is the architecture's genuine adaptive response to category-upgraded threat.

### Prediction VI — Coalition Amplification Asymmetry

Name-calling deployed through coalition should show significantly higher adoption rates than narrative inversion alone, because psychiatric characterization invokes universal harm-prevention logics rather than side-taking. Coalition members who rejected HEA narrative inversion should show >70% acceptance of "I'm concerned about their mental health" framing. Amplification creates double-bind: outlier's assertion of their experience is processed as evidence of the pathology being described (their "obsessive rumination," "inability to accept reality," "distorted perception"), making the resistance evidence of the diagnosis. Coalition confirmation of psychiatric characterization should strengthen suppressor's belief in the characterization's accuracy, creating dissonance-reduction loop.

### Prediction VII — Aggressive-Care Formulation Deployment Timing

ACF emergence should occur after HEA triple-layer has been deployed and outlier has continued resistance beyond 2-3 exchange rounds. ACF structure should show measurable shift from suppressor-as-accurate-perceiver positioning to suppressor-as-concerned-party positioning. Frequency of "I care about you," "I'm worried," "I want to help you" formulations combined with pathology-characterization should increase measurably in direct suppressor-outlier interaction following coalition-amplified name-calling. Suppressor's subjective experience should be that they are *finally* offering help the outlier desperately needs, not that they are escalating aggression.

### Prediction VIII — Return's Failure State Emotional Trajectory

Suppressor's affect trajectory should follow arc: (weeks 1-3 post-exit) satisfaction and certainty; (weeks 4-8) increasing irritability and preoccupation; (weeks 9-12) genuine distress at outlier's "refusal to accept reality"; (post-return-initiation) frustration escalating through active engagement phases, peaking when outlier continues resistance despite suppressor's HEA deployment and name-calling. Suppressor should experience escalation as emotionally necessary rather than strategically chosen — emotional urgency should increase, not decrease, as resistance continues. Suppressor's subjective report should center on concern for outlier and confusion at outlier's irrationality, not on strategic advantage-seeking.

### Prediction IX — Narrative Authority Dependency Behavioral Signature

Suppressor should demonstrate measurably reduced interest in outlier's actual activities/status and measurably increased interest in outlier's *beliefs about what happened*. Suppressor's direct comments to outlier should shift from behavioral criticism to epistemic criticism: "You're remembering wrong," "You don't understand what really happened," "Your perception is distorted" should increase relative to "You did X" or "You are Y." Suppressor's emotional state should show marked improvement when outlier appears to accept narrative inversion ("She finally understands") and marked deterioration when outlier rejects it ("She's impossible"). This demonstrates that suppressor requires validation of narrative more than they require outlier's actual change in behavior.

### Prediction X — IG Restructuring Through Return Phase

The Incompleteness Guarantee's structural configuration should undergo phase-shift through return and escalation. Pre-return IG: outlier's testimony processed through coalition-installed frames as caricature-confirmation. Post-return IG: outlier's testimony processed as evidence of psychiatric pathology. The restructured IG (IG-2) should show measurably higher impermeability to counter-evidence, because psychiatric pathology-characterization is resilient to behavioral disconfirmation ("of course she's being nice now; she's trying to convince people she's not unstable"). Post-return IG should produce distinct phenomenological signature in outlier: not "they won't listen to me" but "they're interpreting everything I do as proof of my mental illness."

## IX. STRUCTURAL REVERSAL

The Return and Revision reveals an inversion in the prior framework's logic:

**Prior assumption:** Exit completes the architecture by transitioning it to autonomous operation.

**Revealed reality:** Exit creates a new form of incompleteness. The autonomous architecture cannot provide what the architecture was actually built to produce: the suppressor's continuous self-reconstitution through direct performance of accurate perception.

**Consequence:** The suppressor's exit from direct engagement becomes, through JPSC_c decay and the need for narrative validation, the condition for the suppressor's return under new structural conditions.

**Terminal state:** The suppressor returns not to suppress but to be validated. The escalation that follows is the suppressor's genuine experience of crisis when validation is not forthcoming. The name-calling is not calculated. It is the architecture's adaptive response to a category of resistance it was not designed to process.

The suppressor who exits believing they have achieved independence from the outlier's significance has achieved only a partial form of independence. They are independent of the outlier's presence. They remain dependent on the outlier's acceptance of the narrative that justifies that independence.

Bandura specified: "The agent believes what they believe. The architecture produced the belief. The architecture and the agent have become indistinguishable." At the return phase, a deeper level of indistinguishability has been reached: the suppressor cannot distinguish between their genuine belief that the outlier is gaslighting them and their structural need to believe it in order to justify their own escalation.

## X. THE TERMINAL IRONY

The Performed Terminus was theorized to complete the suppression architecture by removing the need for the suppressor's continued direct participation. The Return and Revision specifies what that theory could not see: the architecture was not built to make the suppressor unnecessary. It was built to make the suppressor permanently necessary to themselves.

The suppressor who performs the exit is performing an act whose success depends entirely on the suppressor's belief in the exit's justification. When the outlier questions that justification, the suppressor experiences it not as disagreement but as a threat to the foundation of their own self-understanding. The suppressor returns to restore this foundation. The suppressor escalates when the foundation cannot be restored through available means.

The suppressor does not return to harm the outlier. The suppressor returns because the suppressor's harm to themselves — the internal collapse of JPSC_c — is intolerable. The name-calling is not calculated strategy. It is genuine distress converted into psychiatric characterization by the Moral Disengagement Architecture, producing authentic concern for the outlier's "mental health" while simultaneously attacking the outlier's reality-testing capacity at maximum intensity.

This is the machinery's deepest expression: not that it produces harm while appearing to do good, but that it produces harm that is, from the inside, experienced as genuinely good. The suppressor who is aggressively name-calling the outlier as psychologically disturbed is, in their own experience, finally being honest about what they have always known and what the outlier's persistent denial only confirms.

The machinery does not fail when the exit is questioned. The machinery escalates. And the escalation is the machinery at its most complete, most genuine, and most destructive.
